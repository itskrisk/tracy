# Vault Email Templates for Supabase

These templates match Vault's Apple x Neo-brutalist aesthetic with high-contrast borders and responsive layouts.

## How to Set Up in Supabase

1. Open your [Supabase Dashboard](https://supabase.com/dashboard)
2. Go to **Authentication** &rarr; **Email Templates**

### 1. Confirm Signup / Email Change
* Click on **Confirm signup** or **Change Email Address**
* Subject: `Confirm Your Vault Email Address`
* Paste the contents of `confirm-email.html` into the Body editor
* Click **Save**

### 2. Reset Password
* Click on **Reset Password**
* Subject: `Reset Your Vault Password`
* Paste the contents of `reset-password.html` into the Body editor
* Click **Save**
